About

Where logic meets
creative instinct

I'm Suha — a developer who grew up drawing before learning to type, which means I've always thought visually first and technically second. That dual perspective shapes every project I take on: the code is purposeful, and the design has intention behind every pixel.

By day I build responsive, accessible web applications. By evening I'm behind a camera or in front of a canvas, exploring the same questions about form, light, and emotion — just with different tools.

Journey

How I got here

  1. 2018

    Started painting seriously

    Enrolled in a local art programme and began exhibiting watercolours in small gallery shows. Art taught me to see before I learned to build.

  2. 2019

    First line of code

    Built a personal blog in raw HTML and CSS to showcase artwork. That one site sent me down a rabbit hole I haven't climbed out of.

  3. 2020

    CS degree begins

    Started a Bachelor's in Computer Science. Worked as a freelance web designer simultaneously, funding studies with design contracts.

  4. 2021

    First internship

    Junior front-end intern at a Dhaka-based product studio. Shipped features used by 50k+ users in my first three months.

  5. 2022

    Photography takes hold

    Bought a second-hand mirrorless camera. Street photography became a weekly practice — learning to slow down and notice is still my best programming lesson.

  6. 2023

    Graduated & went full-stack

    Graduated with distinction. Joined a remote startup as a full-stack engineer and shipped Chronicle CMS, which went open-source.

  7. 2024 — Now

    Code & Canvas, independent

    Running my own practice — client work in the week, personal art at the weekend. Looking for collaborators who care about both craft and impact.
